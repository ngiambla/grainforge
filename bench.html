How well do photos turn into models?

Each piece below is rendered from its true model with known sizes, photographed from the front, the side and at an angle. Every test outlines the piece the way the app does, gives it one real measurement (usually the width), fits a model and compares it with the truth. Sizes the photos never show are marked as guessed.
